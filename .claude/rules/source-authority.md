# Source Authority and Evidence Discipline

Always loaded. Recorded 2026-10-14 when the project was initialised. This project is in its
**research phase**, so most of what it holds is evidence about other organisations. The way that
evidence is labelled is the thing most likely to go wrong.

## Ranking

| Rank | Source | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| ISSUED | AEA Project 1 brief | `docs/source/aea-proj1-onboarding-assistant.md` | Unchanged copy. It is what gets graded. Never edit it; record provenance in `docs/source/source-register.md`. |
| ISSUED (pending) | Gauntlet staff answers | `Gauntlet AI/open-questions.md` (open GQ-18 and GQ-20) | When one arrives, it outranks our assumptions. GQ-19 was resolved from first-party disclosures. |

| DERIVED | Our analysis | `docs/analysis/` | Judgments. Never cite our own analysis as if it were a company fact. |

## Rules

1. **Separate verified fact, inference and unknown.** Every company profile has the three sections.
   A fact names its source URL and the date it was read. An inference says what it rests on. An
   unknown links an `RQ-N` in `docs/analysis/research-questions.md`.
2. **A website label is not control.** "Current" on AEA's portfolio page establishes a public
   listing. It does not establish control, procurement access, a buyer, or permission to deploy.
3. **A practice or branch is not an AEA investment.** Count platforms (four core + one adjacent).
   Affiliated practices and locations are possible reuse sites, not extra investments or
   confirmed deployments.
4. **One practice's page is evidence for that practice.** Never generalise one affiliated
   practice's intake steps to its whole network.
5. **An existing tool is not a failure.** Don't frame the product as replacing or fixing a
   company's current form, portal or chatbot unless evidence shows a gap.
6. **The niche is a research choice, not a requirement.** The brief targets general
   sensitive-service onboarding; healthcare is optional. Administrative patient intake is our
   chosen first niche, not an approved PRD or a validated customer need.
7. **Administrative, not clinical.** Intake summaries are never diagnosis, urgency triage,
   insurance approval or a referral bypass. Booking respects prerequisites.
8. **No real personal data.** Research cites public pages only. Samples and golden sets are
   synthetic (`synthetic-test-data.md`).
9. **Each fact has one owner.** Link to it; don't restate it in a second file. The owner map is in
   `.claude/README.md`.
10. **Riverside is out of frame for this AEA research.** The portal partner identity is The
    Riverside Company, legally Riverside Partners L.L.C. in its own disclosures (GQ-19 resolved
    2026-10-15). Shared-submission permission remains GQ-18. Neither Riverside nor the separate
    Boston Riverside Partners/RS2 firm drives this project's framing.
