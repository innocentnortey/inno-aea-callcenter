## 1. Verified public facts (as recorded 2026-10-13/14)

**Investment strategies (S-7, S-8):**
- AEA invests through several strategies:
  - Middle Market Private Equity, which focuses on industrials and industrial services;
  - Enterprise;
  - AEA Elevate;
  - Private Debt.
- Elevate covers both technology-enabled business services and healthcare services. Its named
  areas include advisory, legal, IT, recruiting, physician practices and behavioural health.

**People and programmes:**
- AEA has a **Head of AI Solutions, Avi Shah**, formerly a product director for Salesforce
  Agentforce (S-9). His remit, as described: find AI opportunities and "lead end-to-end
  implementations across the portfolio".
- Elevate's value-creation framework, SLATE, has a technology pillar described as "AI-enabled
  workflow transformation" (S-7/S-24, per the 2026-10-13 notes).
- Ravi Sarin is named as Head of AEA Elevate on Commonwealth's AEA profile (S-10, read 2026-10-14).

**The supplied brief does not say which AEA team is hiring** (RQ-1).

## 2. The public portfolio directory, counted (S-6, S-4)

AEA's [portfolio directory](https://www.aeainvestors.com/portfolio/) linked **118 profiles** on
2026-10-14. Each profile shows a status and a group.

| Status · group | Profiles | Counted as a current AEA listing? |
|---|---|---|
| Current · Elevate | 9 | Yes |
| Current · Middle Market Private Equity | 23 | Yes |
| Active · Middle Market Private Equity | 1 | Yes |
| Current · Enterprise | 23 | Yes |
| Current · AEA Elevate Team Prior Experience | 3 | **No.** These are the team's prior experience, not AEA investments. |
| Realized (all groups, incl. 4 team-prior-experience) | 59 | **No** |
| **Total** | **118** | **56 current/active listings** |

**What these labels mean, and what they do not:**
- "Current" and "Active" are website labels.
- They do not establish control, procurement access, a buyer or permission to deploy.
- A Current label may cover a minority or retained stake.
- The directory may be incomplete or stale.
- Private-debt borrowers were not treated as businesses AEA can roll a product into.

**The nine current Elevate listings:**
- American Dental Companies, American Oncology Network, Commonwealth Pain & Spine and Metro Vein
  Centers. These four are the patient-facing core.
- Mark Spain Real Estate, 829 Studios, AS Software, Trinamix and Bespoke Partners.

**Excluded from current counts although health-related:**
- Cenegenics is Current, but only as team prior experience.
- Oceans Healthcare, Riviera Partners and The Oncology Institute are team prior experience
  and realized.
- AS Software (clinical software) and Impetus Wellness Group were not counted as patient-facing
  platforms merely because they work in health-related markets.

## 4. Our interpretation (inference)

- **Role hypothesis.** The role is an applied AI engineer who ships workflows that move
  portfolio-company business metrics. That fits a Head of AI Solutions whose remit is portfolio
  implementations, and a framework pillar named "AI-enabled workflow transformation". The
  hypothesis is unconfirmed (RQ-1).
- **Healthcare is an example, not AEA's domain.** Elevate spans business and healthcare services,
  and Middle Market is mostly industrial. The healthcare niche is our choice for the brief, not
  something AEA's portfolio imposes.
