# Numotion — Adjacent Fifth Opportunity

**Role in this project:** an adjacent fifth opportunity, and the only candidate outside Elevate. The
front of its journey (who the customer is, insurance, documents, booking the evaluation) could reuse
the intake core. The **equipment workflow** (medical documentation, funding, build, delivery,
repair) is a separate multi-week, multi-party process and needs its own design.
**Sources read:** 2026-10-14 (S-22, S-23); re-read and extended **2026-10-15** (S-23,

## 1. Verified public facts

### 1.1 Footprint and services
- **Branches:** 114 in 45 states (S-105). The Nashville branch is at 161 F Bain Drive, LaVergne TN.
  It is open Mon–Fri 8:30–5:00, and new-equipment evaluation is "available at this Numotion branch
  with an appointment".
- **Services** (S-101): complex rehab technology (power and manual wheelchairs, seating), service
  and repair (in-home, in-shop, remote), urology supplies and catheters, speech solutions (AAC),
  workers' compensation services (CorLife) and retail (SpinLife).
- **People served** (S-101): "people of all ages", including injured workers. Adult and pediatric
  solution pages exist.

### 1.2 Equipment process (published; durations are the company's estimates)

| Step (S-23) | Stated time | What the page says |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Evaluation | about 1 week | Measurement and fitting with an Assistive Technology Professional (ATP); insurance benefits verified |
| 2. Medical documentation | **about 2–4 weeks** | Clinical assessment and justification written; submitted to the funding source |
| 3. Funding approval | about 2–8 weeks | Insurance reviews; requests for more information possible |
| 4. Delivery process | about 2–8 weeks | Build 2–8 weeks ("can vary on supplier and availability"); delivery about 1 week |

- **Insurance page** (S-102): "Once we receive a referral", the process is verification, then prior
  authorisation, then follow-up. "The approval timeline can range from 15 to 60 days." Denials are
  appealed.
- **Customer tips** (S-102):
  - Provide primary *and* secondary insurance up front.
  - Standard Medicare needs the Medicare Beneficiary Identifier (MBI).
  - Medicare covers "one wheelchair every 5 years, unless your medical condition changes".
- **Medical necessity** (S-102): for complex power and manual chairs, step 1 is "a Face-to-Face
  appointment with the Doctor". A Letter of Medical Necessity is required, and each feature must be
  justified individually.
- **Payment** (S-103): "Numotion accepts Medicaid & Medicare in all states and most major medical
  carriers across the country." Online payment, payment plans and CareCredit are available.
- **Contact** (S-104): national customer care (800) 500-9150, live chat and "Track your order".
  After-hours phone support runs Mon–Fri 5 PM–12 AM and Sat–Sun 10 AM–7 PM (EST).
- **Repair** (S-106): request online or by phone. Have the serial number, make and model ready, and
  describe the problem. If parts are needed, Numotion orders them and returns to install.
- **myNumotion app** (S-106): the public app guide lists "Real-Time Order Information",
  "Text/Email notification of order status change", equipment listing, service requests, an
  appointment calendar, self-pay estimate, invoices and live chat with a representative. Its
  home-screen image shows order status, appointments, service requests and a clinical-team entry.
  Registration asks for name, date of birth, phone, email and preferred contact method. **This is
  documented capability from a public guide, not a test of a live customer account.** It does not
  show which order steps produce notifications, how current the status is, or how well it works.

## 2. Our interpretation (inference)

- **The reusable part is the front of the journey:** who the customer is and who is completing
  intake (user, family or clinician), insurance (primary and secondary, the MBI), the referral and
  the physician's face-to-face visit, and booking the evaluation.
- **The equipment workflow is a different product.** It is a status tracker and a document chaser
  across the ATP, the physician, the insurer and the supplier. Numotion already documents status,
  appointment, service-request and team-contact features in its app (S-106), so any proposal there
  must be framed as a complement, validated against that app. Reviews (S-107) describe chasing
  progress on repairs and funding; they concern established customers and do not show whether the
  app was used or where it fell short.

## 3. What we must not assume

- That the clinic configuration covers Numotion's workflow (RQ-12).
- Who completes intake: the end user, a family member, a clinician or a referral source (RQ-16, RQ-12).
- That stated step times are actual times. Reviews (S-107) report longer waits, but those are
  attributed experiences.
