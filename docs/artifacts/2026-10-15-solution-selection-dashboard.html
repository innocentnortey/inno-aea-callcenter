Overview

The business problem

Across the five AEA platforms studied, reviewers usually praise the clinical work. The problems they report sit mostly in the administrative stretch around a visit: getting a call back, knowing what is pending, not repeating details, and arriving at the right time with the right instructions.

The research turns that into four questions a patient needs answered:

  1. Was my information received?
  2. What is missing?
  3. Who handles the next action?
  4. What happens next?

Source: selection matrix §1; review pitfalls, "The strongest product opportunity".

The proposed value (a hypothesis)

Clearer steps, less repeated entry and usable staff handoffs could reduce administrative effort and help more patients reach a first visit. Completion, attendance, retention and revenue are outcomes to measure, not established benefits.

Measured baseline: unknown for every company (RQ-2, RQ-3, RQ-38). The brief's 50% → 70% completion figure is illustrative (S-1). No performance chart is shown because no performance data exists.

What an intake assistant cannot fix on its own: staffing, payer decisions, treatment outcomes, appointment capacity or equipment repairs.

    How the ranking score is calculated

    Each criterion is scored 1–5. Score = Σ(weight × score ÷ 5); maximum 100. This is a research judgement, not measured ROI.

    CriterionWeightWhat 5 means
    E · Evidence strength and directness20Direct reports at ≥2 platforms plus published workflow
    P · Importance of the problem20Blocks or delays getting to a first visit, or damages trust
    F · Fit with the brief and administrative scope20Directly serves a brief requirement or stated problem; no clinical judgement
    D · Demonstrable in the 3-day timebox with synthetic data15A clear pass/fail demo, little extra infrastructure
    R · Reuse across portfolio companies15Applies to all five platforms
    I · Low effort and low integration/operational dependency10Small build; works without company systems or new staff processes

    Evidence strength (E): 5 = direct attributed reports at ≥2 platforms plus published workflow · 4 = direct reports at one platform, or a published requirement at ≥3 platforms, plus guidance · 3 = published workflow or guidance with indirect or single reports · 2 = derived from the brief or our analysis only · 1 = speculative.

    Tie-breaks: SOL-005 ahead of SOL-011 (both 83) on brief fit · SOL-006 ahead of SOL-014 (both 82): a complete solution with stronger evidence, while SOL-014 mainly supplies content for mandatory M-04 · SOL-008 ahead of SOL-018 (76) on evidence · SOL-009 ahead of SOL-012 (75) on evidence and importance · SOL-013 ahead of SOL-017 (63) as a brief stretch item. Sensitivity: the cut between rank 8 and rank 9 is a tie broken by judgement.
