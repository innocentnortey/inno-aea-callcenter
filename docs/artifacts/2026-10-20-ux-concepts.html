The story so far

We are building a chat agent for Meridian Home Comfort’s homeowners, and a staff console behind it. A homeowner whose air conditioner stopped writes to the chat. A representative, Riley in the demo, picks up what the chat could not finish. UX-2 asks how those screens should look.

Where we started Fact

UX-1 drew who uses the product and the paths they take. On 2026-10-20 you chose family A, “dependable dispatch”: competent, organised, on time. The family can be built in many ways, so UX-2 made several, called directions.

DESIGN-DIRECTIONS.md, status lines.

What went wrong Fact

We built clickable prototypes of A1 and A2 before you had seen their colours. You said they looked alike and you did not like either. We then built A3, also as a prototype, before showing you anything.

DESIGN-DIRECTIONS.md status lines; PROJECT-CHECKLIST.md §4b.

What fixed it Fact

From A4 on, each direction was a look sheet: a static page with the screens drawn in that direction’s colours. A look sheet is quick to make, so you could see colour first. A5 came from a shot you pointed at, and you picked it.

DESIGN-DIRECTIONS.md, A4 to A6.

Two words you will see. A direction is a look: colours, shapes and type, like choosing between a clipboard form and a bank statement. A concept is a layout: where things sit on the screen. You choose them separately. The concepts were settled first and did not change.

The lesson: look sheets before prototypes

What we learned Fact

The first three directions each took a full clickable prototype. You judged them on colour and feel, and a prototype is a slow way to show colour. The look sheets for A4, A5 and A6 were saved about ten to fifteen minutes apart (file times 23:29, 23:45 and 23:56), and each one gave you something to react to.

You said it plainly when the zero-to-hero rule was written: “screenshots or fast light mockups before showing me so i can see the colors then you can build the full full protoype”.

The rule now. Every design direction gets a quick static look sheet first: credited references, a light and a dark palette with contrast checked, and three or four mockups of the core screens on real flow copy. Only the direction you pick gets a clickable prototype. Directions must also differ at a glance, in softness, density, shape, colour temperature and type, so two of them are never mistaken for one another. Nothing unbuilt is shown next to built things.

What the pick hands to UX-3

UX-3 writes the information architecture, the screen specs with every state, docs/design/design.md and the signed UI contracts. These items come from the A5 section of DESIGN-DIRECTIONS.md. They are a hand-over list from Claude, not decisions you have made.

Make the colours exact Proposal

  • The shot is light only, so the dark theme is ours. Both themes are designed.
  • The shot’s emerald with white text is 2.49 to 1 and its grey captions about 3.7 to 1. Both fail WCAG AA as used, so A5 darkens the emerald and the greys. UX-3 sets the exact values.
  • Red stays reserved for safety, and the safety card is the only red and the only square object.

Check the two watch items Proposal

  • Mint means two things: the homeowner’s own turns on the phone, and “the assistant wrote this” on the console. They never share a screen, but UX-3 should confirm it, or give the console block a different tint.
  • A5 passes the house-texture check with four watches (items 2, 4, 5 and 7). It is also the most “SaaS help desk” of the five, so the texture has to come from the card construction and the mint and grey split, not the palette alone.

Update the specs Proposal

  • Reply cards become thin-lined cards with tinted header rows. The slot card becomes day tabs with mint-selected tiles. The handoff becomes a “Your request” card with a mono reference.
  • The case view header gains the mono reference, a reason pill and a green “Claim” split button. The promise moves into a mint block marked as written by the assistant.
  • The old A2 mono-caps labels in SCREEN_SPECS.md become sentence-case labels. Times keep your earlier condition: 12-hour, like “7:00 pm”.

Do not carry over from the shots Proposal

  • Thinking-time timers, typing dots and any chat timer or wait estimate: we cannot measure them and the honesty rules forbid them.
  • The notification red, red and green delta numbers, the “Auto Reply” toggle, “Regenerate Reply” and tone controls, and the thumbs ratings.
  • A face or robot for the assistant. Its mark is a plain mint square.
