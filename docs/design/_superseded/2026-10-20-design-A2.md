# Design — Meridian AI service agent (A2 Route sheet)

> **Status:** PROPOSED 2026-10-20 · UX-3 (`ux-pipeline` phases.md) · awaiting the ★ UX-3 gate (Innocent).
> **What this file is:** the chosen direction made exact. It is the single source of truth (UI-2) for the look of the
> customer chat (SCR-1, plus the demo tools SCR-2/SCR-3) and the staff console (SCR-4…SCR-11). It defines tokens,
> type, spacing, motion and the anatomy of every part. It does **not** define screens, regions or states per screen
> (that is `docs/ux/03-structure/SCREEN_SPECS.md`) or data shapes (that is `UI_CONTRACTS.md`).
> **The decision it makes exact (Innocent, UX-2 gate, 2026-10-20):** "lets go with A2 light mode": **direction A2 Route
> sheet, light mode first**, on concepts **C2** (chat + "what's settled" panel) and **S2** (queue → brief → evidence).
> His four conditions are built in, not bolted on:
> 1. **12-hour times everywhere** ("7:00 pm", "8 am – 12 pm"), at A2's headline size and weight (§3.4);
> 2. **mono caps labels paired with a sentence-case gloss**, to soften A2's coldness for a stressed homeowner (§3.5);
> 3. **red only on the safety card** (§4.5);
> 4. **the demo bar and the simulated-staffing banner are neutral**, never warning styling (§8.10).
>
> **Light is primary. Dark is defined and secondary:** every token has a dark value that meets contrast, but dark has
> had less visual polish and is checked last at UX-5.
> **Sources of the look:** [`DESIGN-DIRECTIONS.md`](DESIGN-DIRECTIONS.md) (A2 anatomy, house-texture check, review
> notes, the "do not carry over" list, Dribbble refs dr-01…dr-05 in [`references/`](references/)); the built A2
> prototype [`2026-10-20-direction-A2-route-sheet.html`](../artifacts/2026-10-20-direction-A2-route-sheet.html), sources
> `docs/artifacts/_build/direction-prototypes/src/a2.css`, `a2.skin.js`, `proto.css` (every value here was extracted from
> them, then fixed where §13 says); colour roles from the
> [company profile §7](../analysis/2026-10-20-meridian-company-service-profile.md#7-brand-and-color-direction).
> **Not invented here:** no new aesthetic. Where a part was never built in the prototype (change request, limit notice),
> it is assembled from parts that were, and says so.

**Reading order.** §1–2 say what it should feel like. §3–7 are the tokens (type, colour with computed contrast,

§10–11 are copy tone and project do/don'ts. §12 is the texture check, §13 what changed from the prototype, §14 what

## 1. Feeling & audience

**Dependable · exact · calm.** "Dispatch has this; here is the plan, to the minute."

| Who | Where | What they need from the look |
|---|---|---|
| **Homeowner** (SCR-1) | Phone first, one hand, often at a broken unit, sometimes hot and stressed (profile §6) | The window and the reference are the biggest things on screen; every mono label has a plain sentence under it; safety is unmistakable |
| **Representative / supervisor / owner** (SCR-5…SCR-9) | Laptop first, usable at phone width | Dense, keyboard-driven triage; due times as numbers; facts and claims visibly different; proof next to the story |
| **Reviewer** (all screens, demo tools) | Laptop, both roles side by side | What is real and what is simulated is obvious and calm, never alarming |

**The one impression it must leave:** *someone competent wrote this down, and I can keep it.* The ticket stub is the
physical metaphor for "keep this".

## 2. Aesthetic direction

**Technical-dense, dispatcher's route sheet** (DESIGN-DIRECTIONS A2). Pale cool map paper with hairline routes; the
conversation runs down a route line of stops; facts arrive as perforated ticket stubs; times are the headline; staff
triage on a dark ink board.

## 3. Typography

### 3.1 Families, licences, and the tabular-figure check

| Role | Family | Weights shipped | Licence | Fallback stack |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Body / UI | **Barlow** | 400, 500, 600 | SIL OFL 1.1, © 2017 The Barlow Project Authors (no Reserved Font Name) | `"Barlow", "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif` |
| Display (times, figures) | **Barlow Semi Condensed** | 600, 700 | SIL OFL 1.1, same authors | `"Barlow Semi Condensed", "Arial Narrow", sans-serif` |
| Labels, IDs, metadata | **JetBrains Mono** (variable) | 400–600 | SIL OFL 1.1, © 2020 The JetBrains Mono Project Authors (no Reserved Font Name) | `"JetBrains Mono", ui-monospace, Menlo, monospace` |

- **Tabular figures, verified 2026-10-20 with fontTools** (re-run for this file on the source TTFs and on the prototype's
  woff2 subsets): Barlow's **default digits are proportional** (Regular 350–565 units wide), and its `tnum` feature
  makes them equal (Regular 527, SemiBold 538). Barlow Semi Condensed Bold is the same (default 319–544, `tnum` 521).
  JetBrains Mono digits are all 600 units (monospaced; no feature needed). The prototype's subsets keep `tnum`.
  **Consequence:** `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` is set **on `body`**, not per element, so no time, fee or
  count can be set in proportional digits by accident.
- **Barlow is capped at 600.** Bold (700) is not shipped; anything that asked for 700 in the sans (the prototype's
  invoice total and safety step numbers) uses 600. 700 exists only in the display face.
- **Delivery:** self-hosted woff2, Latin subset (`U+0020-007E, U+00A0-00FF, U+2010-2027, U+2190-2193, U+2212` and the
  other ranges in the prototype's `subset.sh`), features `kern liga tnum lnum case calt`. `font-display: swap`;
  preload only Barlow 400 and Barlow Semi Condensed 700. **Ship each family's `OFL.txt` beside the font files** in the
  real frontend: the subsets drop the licence string from the name table.
- **Fallback metrics:** each fallback gets `size-adjust` / `ascent-override` / `descent-override` computed from the font
  files at UX-4 (fontTools), recorded here when measured. Not guessed now.

### 3.3 The three time styles (A2's headline size and weight, kept)

| Token | Size | Line height · tracking | Where | Example (12-hour) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `--text-time-hero` | `clamp(2.25rem, 9cqi, 3.25rem)` = 36–52px | 1 · −.01em | Confirmation stub | **8 am → 12 pm** |
| `--text-time-case` | `clamp(2rem, 6cqi, 2.75rem)` = 32–44px | 1 · 0 | Brief header (staff) | **Mon Oct 19 · 7:00 pm** |
| `--text-time-due` | `clamp(1.75rem, 7cqi, 2.375rem)` = 28–38px | 1.05 · 0 | Handoff stub, change request | **Mon Oct 19 · 7:00 pm** |

All three are **Barlow Semi Condensed 700, tabular**, exactly A2's sizes and weight. Sizes use container units, so
they scale with the stub, not the window.

**The meridiem ("am", "pm").** In the three display styles and the figure styles, "am"/"pm" is a `<span class="mer">`
at **0.6em, same weight, lowercase**, after a normal space, sitting on the baseline. It keeps the numerals at the
headline size and stops the suffix doubling the line's width on a phone (dr-02 sets its "MIN" suffix the same way).
In mono styles (`--text-meta`, `--text-window`) the meridiem stays full size, so columns still align.

**Width check at 390 px** (stub content ≈ 290 px wide, so the hero clamps to 36 px): "8 am → 12 pm" ≈ 4.5em ≈ 162 px;
"Mon Oct 19 · 7:00 pm" at 28 px ≈ 225 px. Both fit on one line.

### 3.4 12-hour time rules (condition 1)

| Context | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Arrival window (a range) | `h am – h pm`; minutes only when not on the hour; the meridiem on **both** ends; en dash with spaces | 8 am – 12 pm · 12 pm – 4 pm · 4 pm – 7 pm |
| Window as a hero | the same, with `→` in place of the dash | 8 am → 12 pm |
| A point in time (due, asked, event) | `h:mm am`, always with minutes | 7:00 pm · 2:14 pm |
| Date + time, short | `Ddd Mmm d · h:mm pm` | Mon Oct 19 · 7:00 pm |
| Date + time, in a sentence | as the policy table writes it | by Monday, October 19, 7:00 pm |
| Staff rows (mono) | `Ddd h:mm pm` | Mon 7:00 pm · Fri 2:14 pm |
| Noon and midnight | `12 pm`, `12 am` | never "noon", never "0:00" |
| The demo date chip | `Ddd Mmm d, yyyy (demo date)` | Fri Oct 16, 2026 (demo date) |

- **Never `text-transform: uppercase` on an element that contains a time or a date.** Mono caps are for labels only;
  "7:00 PM" and "MON OCT 19" are both wrong.
- **Machine-readable:** every time is a `<time datetime="2026-10-19T19:00-05:00">` (Austin, CDT until Nov 1, 2026).
  A hero arrow is `aria-hidden`, with a visually hidden "to".
- **No 24-hour string reaches the screen.** Contracts carry ISO datetimes; formatting is the frontend's job (§14,
  item 4).
- Times shown are the scheduling DB's and the policy table's, never the model's (DESIGN-DIRECTIONS, "What stays fixed").

### 3.5 Label + gloss (condition 2)

A2's mono caps label stays: it is the construction (dr-05's TIME / CLINICIAN / ROOM grid, dr-03's metadata lines). On
the customer chat it never stands alone. **Every block-level label on SCR-1 is paired with a sentence-case gloss** that
says the same thing the way a person would.

```
YOUR VISIT                       ← --text-label, --color-text-muted, aria-hidden when a gloss exists
Booked and on the schedule.      ← --text-gloss, --color-text-muted, the accessible text
8 am → 12 pm                     ← the value
```

- **Placement:** the gloss sits directly under the label, `--space-0-5` (2 px) apart, left-aligned, and wraps freely.
  Together they count as one header; the value follows after `--space-1-5` (6 px).
- **Screen readers** hear the gloss, not the letters: the mono label gets `aria-hidden="true"` when a gloss is present.
- **Cell labels** inside a field grid (DATE, TECHNICIAN, FEE) are one plain word and take no gloss; the block's gloss
  covers them (UI-1: a gloss under "FEE" would only repeat it).
- **Speaker labels** on message rows are names, not jargon, and take no gloss; the chat header says "Meridian assistant
  · automated" in sentence case.
- **Staff console:** glosses are used on column headers and where a label is not self-evident (CHANGED SINCE, NOT
  CHECKED); trained staff read the labels alone elsewhere.

**Gloss vocabulary for SCR-1** (copy notes; final wording is checked at UX-3's screen specs):

|---|---|
| WHAT'S SETTLED | Fills in as things are confirmed. |
| YOUR VISIT | Booked and on the schedule. · empty: *Nothing booked yet.* |
| YOUR REQUEST | Passed to a person. · empty: *No request open.* |
| REF | Your reference. Quote it if you get in touch. |
| DUE | A representative gets back to you by |
| FOLLOW-UP | How we'll reach you |
| IDENTITY | Only needed for private details |
| ARRIVAL WINDOWS | The technician arrives any time in the window you pick. |
| STANDARD FEE | What the visit costs |
| KEEP THIS | Your visit details, to save or screenshot |
| VERIFY | So we only show your details to you |
| VISIT ADDRESS | Where the technician goes |
| INVOICE | Your bill, as we have it on record |
| CHANGE REQUEST | Waiting for a representative to confirm |

### 4.5 Colour rules (conditions 3 and 4, profile §7)

1. **Red is the safety card's alone.** `--color-safety` may be read by the safety card (and its pinned phone copy) and
   by no other component. These do **not** use red: errors (ink "!" block + text), overdue (clock icon + "Overdue"),
   P0 in the queue (light block + "Safety"), the demo bar, the banner, destructive actions, and focus. A lint rule at
   UX-4 fails the build if any other selector reads `--color-safety`.
2. **The demo bar and banner are neutral** (steel only): no amber, yellow, orange or red; no warning triangle; no hazard
   stripes or hatching; no pulsing. "Simulated" is a fact to state, not a hazard (§8.10).
3. **Status is never colour alone.** Every state is a word plus a shape or fill:
   - stops pair a disc state with a chip word (Completed / Next / Scheduled);
   - priority pairs a block fill with its code and word;
   - claims pair a hatch and a hollow marker with NOT CHECKED;
   - changed values pair a filled CHANGED SINCE block with bold text.
4. **Cobalt is the only accent.** It means "selected, done, current, or a person". It never fills large areas: the
   largest cobalt fill is a selected key.
5. **No hex in components.** Components read semantic or component tokens only.

## 6. Motion

Motion is used for exactly two jobs: **show the next action** and **show where a fact landed**. Nothing else moves.
All motion is written as an opt-in under `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`.

| Token | Value |
|---|---|
| `--dur-control` | 150ms (background and colour changes on keys, rows, tabs, options) |
| `--dur-toast` | 200ms (toast fade) |
| `--dur-arrive` | 240ms, with `--delay-arrive` 80ms (a new stub tears in) |
| `--dur-route` | 320ms (the route line draws to the new stop) |
| `--dur-landed-hold` / `--dur-landed-fade` | 400ms / 800ms (the "landed here" ring) |
| `--ease-enter` | `cubic-bezier(0, 0, .58, 1)` (CSS `ease-out`) |
| `--ease-exit` | `cubic-bezier(.42, 0, 1, 1)` (CSS `ease-in`) |
| `--ease-move` | `cubic-bezier(.42, 0, .58, 1)` (CSS `ease-in-out`) |
| `--distance-arrive` | 6px |

| Moment | What moves | Why (which job) | Reduced motion |
|---|---|---|---|
| A new stop arrives in the thread | The newest route segment draws downward (`transform: scaleY(0 → 1)`, origin top, `--dur-route`, `--ease-enter`) | Leads the eye to the new turn: the next thing to read | No draw; the segment is simply there |
| A stub arrives | `opacity 0 → 1` + `translateY(6px → 0)`, `--dur-arrive`, delay 80ms (dr-02's "new window sent") | Shows the card holding the next action | Appears instantly |
| A fact lands in the C2 panel (booking, handoff, identity) | A 3px `--color-accent` ring on a `::after` layer: held 400ms, then `opacity → 0` over 800ms | Shows where the fact landed (CONCEPTS C2 "Linking") | **The ring still shows**, static, and is removed after 2s with no fade: the information is kept, only the motion goes |
| A brief source tag is clicked (S2) | The evidence column scrolls to the source (`behavior: smooth`), then the same landed ring | Shows where the proof is | Instant scroll; static ring for 2s |
| Key, row, tab or option state change | `background-color`, `color` over `--dur-control` | Confirms input was received | Instant |
| Key press | `translateY(1px)` and the keycap edge collapses, 0ms | Tactile press | Kept (1px, user-initiated) |
| Toast | `opacity` over `--dur-toast` | Confirms a quiet action | Instant |

**Never:** bouncing, pulsing, spinners that spin forever, attention loops on the safety card, typing indicators for the
assistant (DESIGN-DIRECTIONS A3 "do not carry over"), animated countdowns. Only `transform` and `opacity` animate; the
prototype's `box-shadow` keyframe for the highlight becomes the `::after` opacity ring (§13).

## 7. Dark theme (secondary)

- **Mechanics:** light tokens on `:root`; dark overrides under
  `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark) { :root:not([data-theme="light"]) { … } }` and again under
  `:root[data-theme="dark"]`; `color-scheme: light dark`. The prototype's toggle mechanism is kept.
- **Base surface** `#0B1117` (dark steel, not black). **Elevation** steps lighter: `#141E28` → `#1B2733`.
- **The board** goes darker than the canvas (`#060A0F`) on purpose: it reads as a recessed well (dr-04's dark command
  board), not a raised card. It is near-black. That is accepted only because the board is a minority of the screen;
  if it vibrates at UX-5, raise it to steel-925.
- **Accent desaturated and lightened:** cobalt `#2449C7` → `#8FA9FF`; links → `#B3C4FF`.
- **Safety red lightened** to `#CC2530`, so the sheet separates from the dark header (3.11:1) while white text keeps 5.42:1.
- **Primary key inverts** to a light key with dark text (`#E4EBF1` / `#0B1117`). It is the brightest element besides
  the safety card, so there is one primary per view and no bloom.
- **Polish owed:** dark has been checked by computed contrast and the prototype's 1280/390 screenshots only. UX-5's
  light/dark screenshot pass is where it is finished.

## 10. Copy tone for states

- **Voice:** a dispatcher who is on your side. Short sentences, lead with the one thing to know or do, numbers in
  plain digits.
- **Empty:** says what goes there and that it fills on its own ("Nothing booked yet."). In the staff queue, the cleared
  kind: "You're all caught up."
- **Errors:** what happened, why, what to do, in one sentence, next to the field ("That code doesn't match. 2 tries
  left."). Never "Something went wrong"; never red.
- **Success:** quiet. A done footer in past tense with a check, a toast for small actions, the landed ring in the
  panel. No green banners.
- **Promises:** only the policy table's text, word for word; never a countdown, queue position or wait estimate.
- **Demo:** factual and calm: "Simulated", "(demo)", "recorded, not sent".

## 11. Do / Don't (project rules; they override the general `ui-*` defaults where they differ)

**Do**
- Make the window and the reference the largest type on any customer screen they appear on.
- Pair every block-level mono label on SCR-1 with a gloss.
- Use `<time>` with an ISO `datetime` for every time, displayed in 12-hour form.
- Keep the safety card plain, red, verbatim and pinned.
- Show status as word + shape + fill.
- Read semantic tokens only.

**Don't**
- Use red, orange or amber anywhere but the safety card, including for errors, overdue, P0 or the demo surfaces.
- Uppercase a time or date; show 24-hour times; show a countdown.
- Add bubbles, avatars or a typing indicator for the assistant.
- Use radii above 4px, coloured left borders, outlined pills, or a grid of identical cards.
- Animate anything that isn't the next action or a fact landing; animate `box-shadow`, width or position.
- Put a primary key on the board and in the stub of the same region.
