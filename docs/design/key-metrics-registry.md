**Who owns what.** The [framing doc §2](../delivery/2026-10-20-angle-and-demo-framing.md) owns the metrics tree, the
decision lines and the signed targets (Innocent ✓ 2026-10-20). This registry **adds** what the tree leaves open: the
exact formula, the numerator and denominator, the population and segments, the source field, who reads each number and
which decision it informs, its label, and when it is reviewed. Every target below is copied from its owner with the
decision that approved it; where this registry and the framing doc differ, the owner's latest decision is named.
Records and fields are defined in [`observability-contract.md`](observability-contract.md) (DO-6); suites and cases in
[`evaluation-plan.md`](evaluation-plan.md) (DO-5).

**Number labels:** [T] a target Innocent set · [A] an assumption, with what would measure it · [C] calculated · [M]
measured.

## One metric, worked through

**Input.** The sealed held-out split has (say) 40 cases; its gold labels mark 24 in scope. **Process.** The eval runner
plays each case; the checklist marks 22 of the 24 in-scope cases "resolved correctly with no human". **Output.** M-1 =
22 ÷ 24 = 91.7%, shown as "22 of 24 in-scope held-out cases", with the run ID, split, agent version and the min–max
across 3 repeats. Per topic, the same count runs for booking, membership, billing questions and warranty; a topic under
90% goes to error analysis on its dev cases. (The 40, 24 and 22 are illustration only, not a result.)

## Where this registry and other documents differ

1. **Latency line.** The framing doc's health list still shows "p95 above 8 s (assumption)". Innocent replaced it on
   2026-10-21, after SP-1, with **p95 ≤ 12.5 s at 20 conversations at once** (PRD §5; SP-1 §4). M-23 uses 12.5 s. The
   framing doc's owner should update its health row; the draft monitoring copy in SCR-10 ("p95 latency is over 8 s")
   has the same stale number.
2. **An early spend warning.** SCR-10's draft copy shows an alert at "over 80% of the $30 daily cap". No 80% line was
   approved; M-26 alerts only at the cap until Innocent sets an earlier one.
3. **The north star on live traffic.** CON-18's draft names "in-scope correct resolution" on stored conversations.
   Live conversations have no gold label, and the framing doc forbids the agent's own label deciding scope. M-3 is
   therefore a separate, labelled estimate; the north star itself (M-1) comes only from the held-out split.
4. **Definitions, decided by Innocent (2026-10-21):** abandonment ends a chat after 30 minutes (M-18); the zero-traffic
   alert fires after no new live conversation for **4 hours**, not counted during late-night hours (Innocent, 2026-10-21: "4 hours outside of the late night hours"); late night = 10pm–6am Austin time [A, a config value he can change] (M-22); the spend-cap day runs midnight to midnight Austin time (M-26).
