**Date:** 2026-10-21. **What this is:** a consistency audit of the five Stage 7 contract addenda
(`docs/design/contracts/stage7/{models,data,cases,web,staff}.md`) and their ledgers (`docs/plans/7b-decisions/<topic>.md`),
read in full. The graph addenda (`stage7/graph-*.md`) were still being written and are not covered. **No addendum was
edited.** Each row names the one owner whose text wins, the exact wording or shape the others adopt, and the basis.

**How the clashes arose.** The five resolvers ran in parallel and each read the others "as they stood". So web.md and
staff.md each cite names the other no longer uses (MC-1), and several cross-topic notes describe an earlier draft of a
sibling (MC-7, MC-9, MC-13). Where an addendum quotes a sibling, the sibling's current text was checked, not the quote.

verified; two of them, MC-7 and MC-9, turned out partly stale, as their rows say). **For Innocent: none new.** MC-24
adds one consequence to his open DAT-4 question; it is noted there, not asked as a new decision.

**Apply order.** MC-1 → MC-2 → MC-3 first (they rename the types most other rows use); then the rest in any order.
