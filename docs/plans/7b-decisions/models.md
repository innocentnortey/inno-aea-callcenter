## For Innocent

**None this round.** Every item is an interface shape, an internal rule, or a number a measurement can replace. None
changes what is built, what is spent, what the customer is told, or which risk is accepted. Two items come close, and
here is why they stay engineering:

- **MOD-13 (when the provider-cap circuit releases).** The customer gets the same spend-cap handoff whichever rule is
  chosen. The rule only decides how fast a *false* trip heals (10 minutes) and costs one failed request every 10
  minutes during a real cap.
- **MOD-11.** It follows DO-1 §8.2's decided rule (our cap is checked before each turn, and a running turn may
  overrun). It does not choose a new one.

**One human step, not a decision.** To turn trace export on, this app's project has to exist on the VPS Langfuse, and
its project ID goes into `LANGFUSE_PROJECT_ID` (MOD-6). Until then the null sink runs, as SDQ-3 already says.

**Two notes for other owners** (not MOD items; for Innocent's awareness when the web and data resolvers report):
- The Sonnet-call brake of **6** (proposal, AEA-01) equals the most Sonnet calls SP-1's mix B made in one turn: 6, in 7
  of 192 turns [M] (`var/sp1-runs/*-B-live-*.json`). A turn that needs a seventh call halts into a handoff. MOD-12 keeps
  retries out of the count, so the brake is not reached any sooner.
- With up to 30 turns in flight (SDQ-9), each admitted just under $30, the day can overrun by up to 30 × $0.039 =
  **$1.17** [C], not "one turn's cost" as DO-1 §8.2 words it.

## Contradictions between sources (reported, resolved above)

1. **Our cap mid-turn.** DO-4 §12's gateway rule refuses when "our cap" is reached. DO-1 §8.2, DO-4 §14's
   `allow_turn` comment and AEA-26's example let an admitted turn finish. Resolved by MOD-11 for DO-1. DO-4 §12's rule
   1 should be re-worded at the next DO-4 revision.
2. **Risk labels.** The slice plan marks AEA-17, 18 and 20 `risk::security`; their ticket files say none (MOD-14).
3. **Spend DDL owner.** AEA-26 says it writes `20_spend.sql`; slice plan §0 gives every DDL file to AEA-10 (MOD-15).
4. **`export_status()` with no argument** (DO-4 §13) cannot be per turn while 30 turns run at once (MOD-9). This is a
   design gap, not a source conflict.
5. **429 without `retry-after`.** Research §4 says "not every 429 carries it", but DO-4 §11 maps every bare Anthropic
   429 to the spend cap. Kept as DO-4 maps it; the false-trip risk is bounded by MOD-13. DO-4's `RATE_LIMITED` comment
   ("429 with retry-after") also left OpenRouter's bare 429 without a kind (MOD-4).
6. **Overrun size.** DO-1 §8.2 says a turn may overrun "by one turn's cost". With 30 turns in flight the bound is
   about $1.17 [C]. Noted above for Innocent; not changed.
