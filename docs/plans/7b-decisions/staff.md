## For Innocent

**None this round.** Every item is an interface shape, an internal rule, or a question a decided source already answers.
None changes what is built, what is spent, what the customer is told, or which risk is accepted. Two come close, and
here is why they stay off your list:

- **STF-19 (one role per login, not per tab).** SCREEN_SPECS SCR-4 assumed `[A]` that each browser tab could hold its own
  role. Your R-48 decision, UI_CONTRACTS and the OpenAPI all put the role on the one login session instead, so two tabs
  in one browser share it. The demo flow you care about still works: the customer chat keeps running in one tab while
  the other tab acts as Riley, because chat routes ignore the role. What changes for a reviewer: to act as Riley *and*
  the supervisor at the same moment they need a second browser or a private window (a separate login). That follows
  from your decision; it is not a new one.
- **STF-1 (claims belong to the role).** If two reviewers share the one demo login and both act as Riley, they share
  Riley's claims. The OpenAPI's assign target (`riley` or `supervisor`) names a role, so it cannot be otherwise without a
  contract change.

## Contradictions between sources (reported; resolved above)

1. **Role per tab vs per session.** SCREEN_SPECS SCR-4 `[A]` "held per browser tab" contradicts UI_CONTRACTS
   *Conventions*, the OpenAPI (one cookie, no per-tab carrier) and R-48. The session wins (STF-19).
2. **`signed_in_at` clock.** web.md §2 types `LoginSession.signed_in_at` as wall clock; UI_CONTRACTS' convention and the
   OpenAPI example put wire times on the demo clock (STF-22). Resolved by an extra stored `signed_in_demo`; web.md keeps
   its wall-clock field for expiry. *Stale after merge (MC-5): `LoginSession.signed_in_at` is now the demo time and
   `expires_at` the wall time; no separate model field.*
3. **CON-18 `as_of`.** cases.md §9 says the reader stamps `as_of` from the wall clock; that holds for CON-19 only.
   CON-18's is demo time (SCR-9, the convention) (STF-22). *Resolved at merge (MC-5): cases.md §9 adopts it.*
4. **`RoleRefusal.needs` order.** web.md §3 says "as listed in x-roles", but its `StaffAccess.actor_for` takes a
   `frozenset` (STF-24). *Resolved at merge (MC-4): a tuple in x-roles order.*
5. **Metrics provider type.** web.md §4 types `get_metrics_reader()` as the concrete `MetricsReader` (AEA-53, wave 3);
   AEA-48 builds in wave 2, so it needs the `MetricsReads` Protocol (STF-17). *Stale after merge (MC-6): web.md §4
   declares `MetricsReads` and `get_metrics_reads()`.*
6. **`demo_reset` reach.** cases.md §7 appends CON-21 `demo_reset` only to cases with a joined live chat; an open view of
   a re-seeded case then never learns of the reset (STF-23, recommendation to CAS). *Stale (MC-7): cases.md already
   appends to every case stream (`Cleared.case_streams`); MC-8 then moves CON-21's start past the last `demo_reset`.*
7. **`90_staff.sql` owner.** AEA-44's ticket claims it; the slice plan gives every schema file to AEA-10 (STF-21).
8. **`RoleNotAllowed` example** omits `reason`; its description says it is always present (STF-20).
9. **Measure ids.** The OpenAPI examples' `Measure.id` values disagree with DO-7 and DO-5; the field's description defers
   to the registry (STF-18).
10. **Live state.** UI_CONTRACTS CON-14 reads five live states; DO-4 and cases.md store only `controller` (STF-14, a gap
    rather than a conflict). *Resolved: cases.md §1.1 adds `live_state` / `live_since`.*
11. **Ownership label.** cases.md names `StaffRole` / `StaffActor` "web topic"; after the split they are this topic's,
    defined in `domain/common.py` (STF-2). *Changed at merge (MC-2): in the new `domain/staff.py`.*
